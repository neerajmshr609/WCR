How to provide meaningful feedback to each other?

The best way to be helpful is to leave at least one comment at each section: At strengths, weaknesses, next steps and links.

It is also very helpful to review at the project level the complete concept of a series and each file individually as well. (optional) Use our different tools to express your opinion more accurately and quicker.

For instance: leave a score at each file, draw on top of image files, resort the order of all files presented in a project and many more.
