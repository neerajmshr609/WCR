  3. (3) Users can fill out questionnaires and create tickets via the Platform to receive support from the registered organizations.
  4. (4) The consultation process follows a multi-stage process:
    1. a) Collection of inquiries: Users fill out questionnaires and create tickets that describe their concerns. In doing so, users consent to the data provided by them in the questionnaires and tickets, even if they contain sensitive or intimate information, being viewed by the consultants working on the Platform for the purpose of pre-selection and allocation of cases. Personal data such as passport copies or identity documents are protected by technical measures (the PII module, which recognizes identification numbers and names of natural persons and restricts access to them) and are not visible at this stage.
    2. b) Ticket system: The inquiries are collected in a ticket system and made accessible to the registered organizations and their consultants.
    3. c) Pre-selection and exchange: The consultants can view the tickets, ask questions, and exchange information about the cases internally. Users consent to this exchange taking place and their data being viewed and discussed by the participating consultants.
    4. d) Takeover by organizations: A registered organization can take over a ticket and start advising the user. By taking over the ticket, the organization agrees to take sole responsibility for the subsequent consultation.
