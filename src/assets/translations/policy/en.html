§ 2 Your rights

You have the following rights in relation to your personal data:

Information (Art. 15 DSGVO): You have the right to receive a copy of the personal data we hold about you. We will provide you with this information within one month of receiving your request.

Correction (Art. 16 DSGVO): You can request the correction of incorrect or incomplete data. We will correct your data immediately.

Deletion (Art. 17 DSGVO): You have the right to request the deletion of your data if it is no longer necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, you have withdrawn your consent or the data has been processed unlawfully. Exceptions apply if processing is necessary to fulfill a legal obligation, for reasons of public interest or to assert, exercise or defend legal claims.

Restriction of processing (Art. 18 DSGVO): You have the right to request that the processing of your data be restricted if you dispute the accuracy of the data, the processing is unlawful, you need the data to assert, exercise or defend legal claims or you have objected to the processing.

Objection (Art. 21 DSGVO): You can object to the processing of your data for reasons relating to your particular situation, provided that the processing is based on the performance of a task in the public interest or on a balance of interests. You also have the right to object to the processing of your data for direct marketing purposes at any time.

Data portability (Art. 20 DSGVO): You have the right to receive your data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format and to transmit this data to another controller without hindrance from us, provided that the processing is based on consent or a contract and the processing is carried out using automated procedures.

To exercise your rights or if you have any questions about data protection, please contact us at: info@wcr.is

§ 3 Data that we process

We collect and process different types of data to provide and improve our services:

3.1. Technical data when visiting the platform:

IP Address: Stored anonymously for security and analysis purposes. Deleted after 7 days at the latest.

Browser type/version: To optimize the display and functionality of the platform.

Operating system: To optimize the display and functionality of the platform.

Time/date of access: To analyze platform usage and troubleshoot problems.

Referrer URL (previously visited page): In order to analyze platform usage, it will be deleted after 7 days at the latest.

3.2. Platform-specific data:

Username and email address: Mandatory information for account creation and communication is stored for the duration of membership.

Password: Stored encrypted (at least 8 characters, at least 1 number, optional special characters).

Content, media and feedback: Data that you voluntarily upload or share will be stored for the duration of your membership or until you revoke it.

Data from questionnaires: Are processed and evaluated anonymously in order to improve our services. Personal reference is excluded. The anonymized results are used for research purposes and can be stored for up to 10 years.

Profile pictures: Are stored on a voluntary basis and can be deleted by the user at any time.

3.3. Cookies:

Session-Cookies: Temporary cookies that are active for the duration of your session and store, for example, feedback data or completed questionnaires from anonymous users for up to 24 hours. They are automatically deleted when you close your browser.

Permanent Cookies: For registered users, we store cookies longer to improve user-friendliness (e.g. login status, language settings). These cookies have a maximum lifespan of 12 months and are then automatically deleted. You can delete cookies at any time in your browser settings.

Easily manage your cookie settings: A clear cookie banner and an easily accessible cookie manager are implemented so you can easily manage your cookie preferences.

3.4. Data transfer:

To registered NGOs: Data will only be passed on to registered NGOs with your express consent, which can be revoked at any time.

Data from NGOs: The name and status of NGOs are stored separately. Employee data is only saved if participation is voluntary and with express consent.

§ 5 AI use and data anonymization

Anonymization: Data from questionnaires are carefully checked and anonymized to prevent conclusions about personality profiles.

AI-Training: We use anonymized data sets to train and improve our AI models (e.g. Llama 3). These models help us optimize our services and offer you a better user experience.

Consent: Before we use your data from the questionnaires for AI training, we will obtain your express consent. This consent is given via a separate checkbox as part of the cookie consent banner (consent management, CMP) when you visit our website for the first time, or via your profile settings if you are registered with us.

Voluntary: Participation in AI training is completely voluntary. You can use our services without restrictions even if you do not give your consent.

Revocation: You can give your consent to AI training revoked at any time with effect for the future. You can revoke your consent using a function in your profile or by sending an informal message to [removed email address].

Personally identifiable information becomes always stored on separate, protected databases and not used for AI training, even if you have agreed to the use.

Fine-tuning models are only performed on local servers to ensure maximum data security.

Use of open source AI models: We prefer open source models to ensure transparency and control over data processing.

Regular audits: AI systems are regularly subjected to internal and external audits to check compliance with data protection regulations and the effectiveness of anonymization measures.

5.2. Storage of consent:

Technology: Your consents (both for cookies and for AI training) are stored in your personal meeting (session) stored on our server. This session is associated with a cookie called "_session_id" which is stored in your browser.

Session cookie: The “_session_id” cookie is a so-called session cookie. It will be automatically deleted when you close your browser. The session data on our server, which contains your consent, is stored for 14 days after your last visit and then automatically deleted.

Additionally: We also store your consent to AI training in our database, linked to your user profile (if available), but separately from your profile data, for the duration of your membership. This storage is done in order to permanently document the consent and to be able to understand it even after the cookie or session data has been deleted. In addition, this is the only way we can take your consent into account across devices, as the session cookie is only stored on the device with which you gave your consent.

Location: The data from the declaration of consent for AI training is stored in our database on the servers of Amazon Web Services (AWS) in Frankfurt, Germany. The session data is also stored on AWS servers in Frankfurt.

5.3. Declaration of consent in the Cookie Consent Banner (CMP):

When you visit our website for the first time, you will see our Cookie Consent banner. There you will also find information about the cookies used separate checkbox for consent to AI training.

The text in the banner reads:

"We only use anonymized data from questionnaires for training in order to train our AI models specifically to the specific needs of refugees and their advisors. By working with experienced advisors, we continually improve our models to address the often unique challenges To better understand this work, we make our training results and fine-tuned models available to the public on platforms such as Hugging Face, GitHub and our website connected world that does not overlook marginalized groups. Can we use your anonymized data for this important project? Further information can be found in our data protection declaration.

Next to it is a checkbox labeled:

"Yes, I agree to the use of my anonymized data for AI training."

Only if you actively check this checkbox and then click “Accept” do you give us your consent to AI training. If you do not check the box, your data will not be used for AI training. You can change your decision at any time in your profile settings.
